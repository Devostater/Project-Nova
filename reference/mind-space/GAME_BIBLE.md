# Mind Space: Game Bible

## Established creative direction (owner discussions, 2025–2026)
- **Mind Space** is a science-fiction first-person shooter and an **Absolute Zero Interactive (AZI)** game property.
- The project originated in Unreal Engine 4 and has been migrated toward Unreal Engine 5. The creator has discussed an alpha/demo as the near-term priority. Do not claim a released build or specific current engine version without checking.
- Opening: the player awakens from cryogenic sleep aboard a space station.
- Roguelike-inspired repeat runs and respawning are central to the concept. A possibility that the player never truly leaves the station was discussed, but is **not locked canon**.
- Procedural/randomized station layouts, objectives interrupting corridors, and teleporters for planetary excursions are planned design directions.
- The broader progression concept includes three main levels leading toward a final boss. Scope may be reduced for the first alpha.

## Gameplay loop and progression
1. Awaken or begin a run on the station.
2. Navigate hostile rooms, encounter roaming enemies, and tackle an objective room.
3. Fight, survive, upgrade, and push toward bosses/extraction.
4. On death, restart a run with some persistent unlocks.

### Progression distinction
- **Persistent across runs (planned):** unlocked weapons and gadgets.
- **Run-limited (planned):** player upgrades such as damage, accuracy, and ammunition capacity.
- **Objective room concept:** hack a terminal through one of three minigames; hacked progress may persist between runs. An alternative is surviving enemy waves.
- **Combat concepts:** dodge roll, cover, grenades, execution of low-health enemies, and movement upgrades such as climbing, faster sprint, and sliding.
- **Enemy direction:** three tiers/colors, archetypes gated by player level, and pressure against remaining exposed in the open.
- **Boss progression:** a miniboss taking damage, followed by an upward elevator with a clear-state light/sound cue, was discussed as an implementation milestone.

## Visual and environmental anchors
- Science-fiction space station; cryopods and soldiers are recurring imagery.
- Rooms should provide gameplay purpose and visual variety, not endless interchangeable corridors.
- Visual specifics, factions, exact palette, and canonical character designs are not finalized here.
