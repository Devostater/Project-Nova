# Mind Space: Prompt Guide

## Mandatory context
Mind Space is AZI's sci-fi FPS property, **not automatically the Project Nova WebXR flight simulator**. Read [GAME_BIBLE.md](GAME_BIBLE.md) first. Distinguish planned features from implemented ones.

## Useful established design anchors
Cryogenic awakening aboard a hostile space station; repeat-run structure; procedural station spaces; objective rooms with hacking or wave survival; weapons/gadgets as longer-term unlocks; per-run stat upgrades; minibosses and movement/combat progression. These reflect owner-discussed design, not confirmed current build state.

## Prompt recipes
**Concept art:** “Create [asset/scene] for Mind Space, a sci-fi FPS set around a hostile space station and cryogenic awakening. Show [specific gameplay function]. Convey readable combat space, distinctive room purpose, and production-friendly detail. Any new factions, lore, palette, or symbols are proposals, not canon.”

**Gameplay design:** “Design a minimal alpha-ready implementation of [feature] in Mind Space. Separate MVP from stretch goals, specify player feedback and failure cases, and preserve the persistent-unlocks versus per-run-upgrades distinction. Do not assert unverified features are already implemented.”

**Narrative:** “Write a proposed story beat consistent with a cryogenic space-station opening and repeat runs. Mark invented backstory, station origins, or characters as proposals.”

## Guardrails
Do not blend Mind Space lore with Project Nova's heat-core economy or starfighter systems unless explicitly asked. Do not invent shipping status, existing code, or assets.
